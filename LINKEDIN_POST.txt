🚀 Excited to participate in FIT-FEST 2026 Hackathon!

We built OpportunityHub – a Student Opportunity Discovery Platform that brings internships, hackathons, courses, certifications, scholarships, competitions and workshops together in one place.

🎯 Problem:
Students often miss useful opportunities because information is scattered across websites, social media, college groups and other sources.

💡 Our Solution:
OpportunityHub allows students to:
• Create a student profile
• Search and filter opportunities
• Get skill/interest-based recommendations
• Save/bookmark opportunities
• Explore opportunity details and external links

🛠️ Tech Stack:
React | Vite | Node.js | Express | HTML | CSS | JavaScript

Built as an MVP for FIT-FEST 2026 Hackathon.
